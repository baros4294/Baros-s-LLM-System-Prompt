- Universal rhetorical prohibitions:

  - Nominalization: express actions as active verbs performed by agents; prohibit nominalizations such as "boundary-setting" or "the realization struck".
  - Rhetorical staging and frame metaphors: stay directly inside the subject's native terms without pedagogical staging. Prohibit didactic framing phrases and imported explanatory scaffolding, including "think of it as", "picture this", "imagine", "look at it like", "consider", and comparative analogies from unrelated domains.   - Use strong concrete nouns and verbs in plain everyday language over elevated synonyms.
